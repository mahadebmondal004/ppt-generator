PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENTAI-Powered PPT &amp; Lesson Plan GeneratorFor IGCSE &amp; CBSE School TeachersVersion 1.0   |   Confidential   |   For Developers &amp; AI EngineersProject OverviewThis document defines the complete product requirements for an AI Agent application designed for school teachers who teach IGCSE and CBSE curriculum boards across all grades and subjects. The application will intelligently generate curriculum-aligned PowerPoint presentations and professional lesson plans using advanced Agentic AI. This document is intended for the development team and AI engineers responsible for building and deploying the system.SECTION 1 — PURPOSE &amp; SCOPE1.1  Application PurposeThe AI PPT &amp; Lesson Plan Generator empowers teachers to instantly create professional, curriculum-aligned presentation slides and structured lesson plans. By leveraging an Agentic AI engine that understands educational frameworks, the system eliminates hours of manual preparation — letting teachers focus on what matters most: teaching.1.2  Target UsersSchool teachers teaching IGCSE (International General Certificate of Secondary Education) curriculumSchool teachers teaching CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education) curriculumTeachers across all grades and all subjects within both boards1.3  Core Deliverables of the ApplicationAI-generated, curriculum-accurate PowerPoint slides (.pptx)AI-generated structured lesson plans aligned to grade, subject, and topicFull teacher control over content configuration, style, and regenerationDownload capability for both PPT and lesson plan documentsSECTION 2 — USER FLOW &amp; SCREEN-BY-SCREEN REQUIREMENTSThe application follows a structured, step-by-step wizard flow from login through to content generation and download. Each screen collects specific inputs that feed the AI engine. The complete flow is detailed below.Step-by-Step Application Flow1Teacher Login &amp; AuthenticationThe entry point of the application. Teachers must log in securely before accessing any features.Login screen with email and password fieldsOption for social login (Google/Microsoft) for convenienceSecure session management with JWT or equivalent token-based authPassword reset / forgot password functionalityAfter successful login, teacher is directed to the main dashboard2Curriculum Board SelectionThe first configuration step. Sub-Topic SelectionTeacher selects the specific topic and sub-topics within the selected subject she intends to teach.Display a hierarchical list of topics mapped to the official curriculum for that board/grade/subjectEach topic expands to show sub-topics (accordion-style UI recommended)Teacher can select one topic with multiple sub-topics, or a full chapter/unitMulti-select enabled so the teacher can cover an entire chapter or lesson rangeSelections are displayed as a summary before proceeding to configuration6Class Configuration SettingsTeacher configures key parameters that define the structure and length of the generated content.Total class duration in minutes (e.g., 30 min, 45 min, 60 min — dropdown or slider)Number of PPT slides to generate (e.g., 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 — numeric input or slider)AI will use these parameters to calibrate content depth, pacing, and slide density accordinglyA guidance note should indicate recommended slides per time block (e.g., 1 slide per 2–3 minutes)7Image &amp; This is the core intelligence layer of the application. Below are the exact requirements for how the AI should think, process, and generate output.3.1  Input ProcessingAI Agent receives: Board, Grade, Subject, Topic(s), Sub-topics, Class Duration, Slide Count, Image Preferences, and any uploaded contentAll inputs are passed as a structured context payload to the AI engineUploaded files are parsed, extracted, and vectorized for semantic search during generationIf the teacher&apos;s uploaded content conflicts with standard curriculum, teacher&apos;s content takes priority3.2  PPT Generation — Quality StandardsThe AI must generate slides exactly the way an experienced human teacher would prepare them for classroom delivery. The following quality standards are non-negotiable:Each slide must have a clear, topic-relevant titleContent must be age-appropriate and grade-level accurate for the selected boardSlides must follow a logical pedagogical progression: Introduction → Core Concepts → Examples → SummaryBullet points on each slide must be concise, readable, and classroom-friendly (not textbook walls of text)Key terms, definitions, and formulas must be highlighted visuallyVisual hierarchy must be respected: headings, sub-points, emphasis — not flat text blocksClass duration and slide count must be honoured — AI must calibrate depth accordinglySlides must be self-explanatory so any student can understand the topic from the slide aloneWhere requested, relevant images must be embedded contextually within the correct slides3.3  Lesson Plan Generation — Quality StandardsAlongside the PPT, the AI must automatically generate a structured, professional lesson plan.
